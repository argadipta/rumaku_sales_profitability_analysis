# Rumaku Sales & Profitability Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes sales and profitability performance to identify the main drivers behind declining revenue and net profit and to provide actionable business recommendations.

The analysis follows an end-to-end data analytics workflow, from data preparation and validation to exploratory analysis, visualization, business insights, and recommendations.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The main objectives of this project are to:

* Analyze sales and profitability performance.
* Identify significant changes in business performance over time.
* Investigate the drivers behind declining revenue and net profit.
* Analyze product category and sales channel performance.
* Provide actionable recommendations based on data-driven findings.

## 🔎 Key Findings

### Revenue & Profitability

* Revenue declined by **59.72% in Q3 2025** compared with Q2 2025.
* Net profit declined by **65.94%** compared with Q2 2025.
* Profit margin decreased by **5.93 percentage points**.

### Product Categories

* **Kasur & Springbed**
* **Pencahayaan**
* **Lemari & Rak**

These three categories contributed approximately **85.6% of the total profit decline**.

### Sales Channel

Online sales remained profitable, but its margin declined from **39.39% to 30.11%**.

### Discount

Average discount increased from **8.19% to 8.74%**, which may have contributed to margin pressure.

---

## 💡 Business Insight

The decline in profitability was strongly associated with a substantial reduction in business scale.

At the same time, several product categories and sales channels amplified the decline in profit, particularly categories that contributed the largest share of the overall profitability deterioration.

---

## 🎯 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following actions are recommended:

1. **Investigate the decline in sales volume** to understand the main factors behind the Q3 revenue contraction.
2. **Review pricing and discount strategies** for categories experiencing the largest profit decline.
3. **Prioritize recovery strategies** for Kasur & Springbed, Pencahayaan, and Lemari & Rak.
4. **Evaluate the declining online-channel margin** and identify products contributing to the deterioration.
5. **Monitor discount effectiveness** to ensure higher discounts generate sufficient additional sales volume to justify the margin impact.
